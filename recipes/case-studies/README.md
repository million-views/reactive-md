# Case Studies

> Complete, production-grade product examples with interactive prototypes

## What Are Case Studies?

Case studies are **in-depth, real-world examples** of complete product interfaces. Unlike templates (which are starting points), case studies are fully-realized examples showing:

- **Complete architecture**: How to structure complex UIs
- **Realistic interactions**: Multi-step workflows, state management
- **Production patterns**: Component organization, local imports
- **Business context**: Why these interfaces matter, what problems they solve

## Browse the Collection

### E-Commerce Product Detail Page
**File:** `e-commerce-pdp.md`

A high-converting product detail page demonstrating:
- Image galleries with thumbnail navigation
- Variant selection (color, size) with visual feedback
- Real-time cart updates and wishlist functionality
- Component architecture with focused sub-components
- State management for complex interactive elements

**When to use:** Building e-commerce UIs, learning product detail page patterns, studying component composition
